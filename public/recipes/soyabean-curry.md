# Ingredients

- 2 cups soya chunks
- 1 medium potato, cut into cubes (optional)
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tbsp mustard oil (or any cooking oil)
- 2 cups water
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)

## Whole Spices

- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- 2 green cardamoms
- 2 cloves

## Spice Powders

- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp garam masala

## For Garnish

- 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
- 1 tsp ghee (optional)

# Instructions

1. Bring about **4 cups of water** to a boil. Add the soya chunks and cook for **5 minutes**.

2. Drain the soya chunks and rinse them under cold water. Squeeze them well to remove as much water as possible. Repeat the rinse and squeeze once more. This helps remove the raw smell and improves the texture.

3. Turn on the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 and press the **Sauté** button.

4. Heat the mustard oil. Add the bay leaf, cumin seeds, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves. Sauté until fragrant.

5. If using potatoes, add them now and sauté for **3–4 minutes** until lightly golden. Remove and set aside.

6. Add the onions and sauté for **6–8 minutes**, until golden brown.

7. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for **1 minute**.

8. Add the tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate, about **5–6 minutes**.

9. Add the turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, salt, and sugar. Cook for **1 minute**, adding a splash of water if needed.

10. Add the squeezed soya chunks (and potatoes, if using). Mix well so they are coated with the masala. Sauté for **2–3 minutes**.

11. Pour in **2 cups water** and stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot to prevent a **Burn** warning.

12. Press **Cancel**. Close and lock the lid, ensuring the pressure release valve is set to **Sealing**.

13. Select **Pressure Cook** or **Manual** mode and cook on **High Pressure for 5 minutes**.

14. Allow a **10-minute Natural Pressure Release (NPR)**, then carefully release any remaining pressure.

15. Open the lid. If the gravy is thinner than you prefer, press **Sauté** and simmer for **3–5 minutes**.

16. Stir in the garam masala and ghee (if using). Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot.

# Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with:
- Steamed Rice
- Roti
- Paratha
- Luchi

# Notes

- Thoroughly squeezing the soya chunks after boiling is the key to removing their characteristic smell.
- Potatoes are optional but are commonly added in Bengali-style soyabean curry.
- For extra flavor, marinate the boiled soya chunks with a pinch of turmeric, salt, and chili powder for **10 minutes** before cooking.
- Add 2–3 slit green chilies while pressure cooking for extra heat.
- Leftovers taste even better the next day as the soya chunks absorb more of the gravy.
