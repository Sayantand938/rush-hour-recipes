# Ingredients

- 1 cup moong dal (yellow split moong) **or** masoor dal (red lentils)
- 3 cups water
- 1 medium tomato, chopped (optional)
- 1–2 green chilies, slit
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- Salt, to taste

## For the Tempering (Tadka)

- 1 tbsp ghee or mustard oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 dried red chilies
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing) *(optional)*
- 1 tsp grated ginger *(optional, but recommended)*

## For Garnish

- 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
- 1 tsp ghee (optional)

# Instructions

### If Using Moong Dal

1. Add the moong dal to the Air Fryer basket and roast at **180°C for 5–7 minutes**, shaking halfway, until lightly golden and fragrant. (Skip this step for masoor dal.)

2. Rinse the roasted moong dal (or raw masoor dal) thoroughly under running water.

3. Add the dal, water, turmeric, chopped tomato (if using), green chilies, and salt to the Nutricook Smart Pot 2.

4. Close and lock the lid, ensuring the pressure release valve is set to **Sealing**.

5. Select **Pressure Cook** or **Manual** mode and cook on **High Pressure**:
   - **Moong Dal:** 8 minutes
   - **Masoor Dal:** 5 minutes

6. Allow a **10-minute Natural Pressure Release (NPR)**, then carefully release any remaining pressure.

7. Open the lid and stir the dal well. If it's too thick, add a little hot water and simmer using **Sauté** mode for 2–3 minutes.

8. For the tempering, press **Sauté** (or use a small pan on the stove). Heat the ghee or mustard oil.

9. Add the bay leaf, cumin seeds, and dried red chilies. Once they begin to crackle, add the hing and grated ginger. Sauté for about **30 seconds** until fragrant.

10. Pour the hot tempering over the cooked dal and mix well.

11. Garnish with chopped coriander and an extra teaspoon of ghee, if desired.

# Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with:
- Steamed Rice
- Aloo Bhaja
- Begun Bhaja
- Alur Dom
- Papad
- Pickle

# Notes

- **Moong dal** has a nutty flavor and is traditionally roasted before cooking.
- **Masoor dal** does not require roasting and cooks more quickly.
- For a thinner, traditional Bengali-style dal, add an extra **½–1 cup** of hot water after pressure cooking.
- Adjust the number of green chilies to your preferred spice level.
- A squeeze of fresh lemon juice just before serving adds a refreshing finish.
