# Ingredients

- 5 tbsp oil
- 5 medium onions, sliced or roughly chopped
- 8 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 2-inch ginger, peeled & chopped
- 12 garlic cloves
- 2–3 green chilies (optional)
- ½ tsp salt (for browning onions)

## Whole Spices

- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tsp cumin seeds
- 2-inch cinnamon stick
- 4 green cardamoms
- 6 cloves

## Spice Powders

- 2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2½ tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 2 tbsp coriander powder
- 2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala (add at the end)
- Salt, to taste

# Instructions

1. **Sauté Whole Spices:** Turn on the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 and select **Sauté**. Heat 5 tbsp oil, then add bay leaves, cumin seeds, cinnamon stick, cardamoms, and cloves. Sauté for **30–45 seconds** until fragrant.

2. **Brown Onions:** Add the onions along with **½ tsp salt** (this draws out moisture and speeds up browning). Cook for **8–10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden brown.

3. **Add Aromatics & Spices:** Stir in ginger, garlic, and green chilies. Sauté for **2 minutes**. Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, Kashmiri red chili powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Cook for **3–4 minutes** until tomatoes start to break down.

4. **Pressure Cook (Speed & Flavor Extraction):** Press **Cancel**. Add **½ cup water** and lock the lid. Select **Pressure Cook (High)** and set for **5 minutes**. Once done, let it natural release for **5 minutes**, then quick release the remaining steam.

5. **Cool & Blend:** Discard the bay leaves and cinnamon stick. Let the mixture cool slightly, then blend until completely smooth using a stand blender or immersion blender.

6. **Final Bhuna (Simmer):** Return the smooth puree to the pot and select **Sauté**. Stir in **1 tsp garam masala** and adjust salt to taste. Simmer for **5–8 minutes**, stirring continuously, until glossy and oil separates along the edges.

7. **Cool & Store:** Press **Cancel** and allow the gravy to cool completely before portioning.

# Storage

- **Refrigerator:** Up to **7 days** in an airtight glass container.
- **Freezer:** Up to **3 months**. Freeze in ½-cup or 1-cup portions (in freezer bags or silicone trays) for easy single-meal use.

# How to Use

Use **1–1½ cups** of the base gravy to quickly pull together popular curries:

- **Paneer Butter Masala:** Reheat gravy with 1 tbsp butter, add paneer cubes, ½ cup water, 2 tbsp cream, and crushed kasuri methi.
- **Chicken Curry:** Sauté chicken chunks in a little oil, add base gravy and water, then pressure cook for 6 minutes.
- **Egg Curry:** Add boiled eggs to reheated base gravy with a splash of water and simmer for 5 minutes.
- **Chole / Rajma:** Combine cooked chickpeas/kidney beans with base gravy, add chole/rajma masala, water, and pressure cook for 3 minutes.

# Key Optimizations

- **Early Salt Trick:** Adding salt to onions early cuts browning time significantly.
- **Pressure Cooking Step:** Pressure cooking for 5 minutes breaks down tomato skin/pulp completely, removes raw acidity, and saves overall sauté time.
- **Garam Masala Finish:** Adding garam masala during the final simmer keeps aromatic oils intact rather than burning them off early.
