## Ingredients

- 1 cup white rice (Basmati, Gobindobhog, or any regular rice)
- 1¼ cups water (for Basmati)
- 1½ cups water (for regular medium-grain rice)
- ½ tsp salt (optional)
- 1 tsp ghee or butter (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice under cold running water **3–4 times**, or until the water runs mostly clear.

2. (Optional) Soak the rice for **15–20 minutes**. Drain well before cooking.

3. Add the rinsed rice to the inner pot of the Nutricook Smart Pot 2.

4. Pour in the appropriate amount of water:
   - **1¼ cups** for Basmati rice
   - **1½ cups** for regular white rice

5. Add the salt and ghee, if using.

6. Close and lock the lid, ensuring the pressure release valve is set to **Sealing**.

7. Select **Rice** mode. If your model does not have a Rice preset, use **Pressure Cook** or **Manual** mode and cook on **High Pressure for 4 minutes**.

8. Once cooking is complete, allow a **10-minute Natural Pressure Release (NPR)**. Then carefully release any remaining pressure.

9. Open the lid and gently fluff the rice with a fork or rice paddle.

10. Let the rice rest for **2–3 minutes** before serving for the best texture.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with:
- Dal
- Alur Dom
- Rajma
- Chole
- Paneer Curry
- Chicken Curry
- Fish Curry

## Notes

- Always rinse the rice well to remove excess starch for fluffier grains.
- Soaking the rice helps it cook more evenly and results in longer, separate grains.
- For softer rice, add an extra **2–3 tablespoons** of water.
- Leftover rice can be refrigerated for up to **2 days** and reheated with a splash of water.
