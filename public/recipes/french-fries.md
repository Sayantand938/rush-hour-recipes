# Ingredients

- 2 medium potatoes (Russet or any starchy potato)
- 1 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp paprika or Kashmiri red chili powder (optional)
- ¼ tsp black pepper (optional)
- ½ tsp garlic powder (optional)

## For Serving

- Tomato ketchup
- Peri Peri seasoning (optional)
- Chaat masala (optional)
- Mayonnaise (optional)

# Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes if desired, or leave the skin on for a rustic style.

2. Cut the potatoes into evenly sized fries, about **1 cm thick**.

3. Place the fries in a bowl of cold water and soak for **30 minutes**. This removes excess starch and helps make them crispier.

4. Drain the fries and pat them completely dry using a clean kitchen towel or paper towels.

5. Toss the fries with the oil until evenly coated.

6. Preheat the Air Fryer to **200°C for 3 minutes**.

7. Arrange the fries in a **single layer** in the Air Fryer basket. Do not overcrowd the basket. Cook in batches if necessary.

8. Air fry at **200°C for 18–22 minutes**, shaking the basket every **5–6 minutes** to ensure even cooking.

9. Once the fries are golden brown and crispy, transfer them to a bowl.

10. Sprinkle with salt and any optional seasonings while the fries are still hot. Toss well to coat evenly.

11. Serve immediately with your favorite dipping sauce.

# Serving Suggestions

Serve with:
- Tomato Ketchup
- Peri Peri Seasoning
- Chaat Masala
- Garlic Mayonnaise
- Cheese Dip

# Notes

- Soaking the potatoes is the secret to crispier fries.
- Dry the potatoes thoroughly before adding oil—any moisture will reduce crispiness.
- Do not season with salt before air frying, as it can draw out moisture and make the fries less crispy.
- A single layer cooks more evenly than an overcrowded basket.
- For extra-crispy fries, air fry for an additional **2–3 minutes** if needed.
- Fries are best enjoyed immediately after cooking.
