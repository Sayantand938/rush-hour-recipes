# Ingredients

- 500 g baby potatoes or 4–5 medium potatoes, peeled and halved
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tbsp mustard oil (or any cooking oil)

## Dry Spices

- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 bay leaf
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- 2 green cardamoms
- 2 cloves

## Spice Powders

- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp garam masala
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)

## For Garnish

- 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
- 1 tsp ghee (optional)

# Instructions

1. Peel and wash the potatoes. If using medium potatoes, cut them into halves or large cubes.

2. Turn on the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 and press the **Sauté** button.

3. Heat the mustard oil until it begins to smoke lightly, then reduce the heat for a few seconds.

4. Add the bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and cumin seeds. Sauté until fragrant.

5. Add the chopped onions and cook for **6–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until golden brown.

6. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for **1 minute**.

7. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and the oil begins to separate, about **5–6 minutes**.

8. Add the turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, salt, and sugar. Cook for **1 minute**, adding a splash of water if needed to prevent sticking.

9. Add the potatoes and stir well so they are evenly coated with the masala. Sauté for **2–3 minutes**.

10. Pour in **1 cup water** for a thick gravy or **1½ cups water** for a thinner curry. Stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot to prevent a **Burn** warning.

11. Press **Cancel** to stop the Sauté function. Close and lock the lid, ensuring the pressure release valve is set to **Sealing**.

12. Select **Pressure Cook** or **Manual** mode and cook on **High Pressure for 5 minutes**.

13. Once cooking is complete, allow a **10-minute Natural Pressure Release (NPR)**. Then carefully release any remaining pressure.

14. Open the lid and press **Sauté** again. Simmer for **3–5 minutes** if you prefer a thicker gravy.

15. Stir in the garam masala and ghee (if using). Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot.

# Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with:
- Steamed rice
- Luchi
- Paratha
- Roti

# Notes

- For richer flavor, fry the potatoes lightly before adding them to the curry.
- Add ½ tsp Bengali bhaja moshla at the end for an authentic touch.
- If using baby potatoes, cook them whole after peeling.
- Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors continue to develop.
