## Ingredients

- 1 kg chicken, cut into medium pieces (bone-in preferred)
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and halved (optional, but traditional)
- 3 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2–3 green chilies, slit
- 3 tbsp mustard oil (or any cooking oil)
- 1½ cups water
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)

### Whole Spices

- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- 3 green cardamoms
- 4 cloves

### Spice Powders

- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1½ tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp black pepper powder (optional)
- 1 tsp garam masala

### For Garnish

- 1 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
- 1 tsp ghee (optional)

## Instructions

### Marinate the Chicken

1. In a large bowl, combine the chicken with:
   - ½ tsp turmeric
   - 1 tsp salt
   - 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste

2. Mix well and let it marinate for **30 minutes**. (Longer is even better.)

### Cook the Curry

3. Turn on the Nutricook Smart Pot 2 and press the **Sauté** button.

4. Heat the mustard oil until it begins to smoke lightly, then reduce the heat.

5. If using potatoes, fry them for **4–5 minutes** until lightly golden. Remove and set aside.

6. Add the bay leaf, cumin seeds, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves. Sauté for **30 seconds** until fragrant.

7. Add the chopped onions and cook for **8–10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until golden brown.

8. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste and sauté for **1 minute**.

9. Stir in the tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate, about **5–6 minutes**.

10. Add the turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, black pepper (if using), and sugar. Cook for **1 minute**, adding a splash of water if needed.

11. Add the marinated chicken and sauté for **8–10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the pieces are well coated and lightly browned.

12. Return the fried potatoes to the pot. Add the slit green chilies and **1½ cups water**. Stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot to prevent a **Burn** warning.

13. Press **Cancel**. Close and lock the lid, ensuring the pressure release valve is set to **Sealing**.

14. Select **Pressure Cook** or **Manual** mode and cook on **High Pressure for 8 minutes**.

15. Allow a **10-minute Natural Pressure Release (NPR)**. Then carefully release any remaining pressure.

16. Open the lid and press **Sauté** if you prefer a thicker gravy. Simmer for **3–5 minutes**.

17. Stir in the garam masala and ghee (if using). Mix gently.

18. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with:

- Steamed Rice
- Jeera Rice
- Luchi
- Paratha
- Roti

## Notes

- Bone-in chicken gives the curry the best flavor.
- Marinating the chicken for **1–2 hours** results in a more tender and flavorful curry.
- Mustard oil provides an authentic Bengali taste, but any neutral cooking oil can be used.
- Add an extra **½ cup water** if you prefer a thinner gravy.
- Leftover chicken curry tastes even better the next day as the flavors continue to develop.
