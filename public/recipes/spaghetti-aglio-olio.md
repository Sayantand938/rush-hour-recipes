# Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp chili flakes
- ¼ cup olive oil
- Salt and parsley to taste

# Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add garlic and chili flakes, sauté until garlic is golden.
4. Drain pasta, reserve ½ cup pasta water.
5. Toss pasta in the pan with the garlic oil, add reserved water if needed.
6. Season with salt, garnish with parsley, and serve hot.
